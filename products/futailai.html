猫传染性腹膜炎（FIP）口服结合蛋白实验室治疗观察Laboratory treatment observation of oral receptor-binding protein for Feline Infectious Peritonitis (FIP)

背景Background

猫传腹病毒（FIPV）感染猫，实验室记录 5 例治疗前后 CT 值变化：案例1精神不佳 CT 33.1(+)；案例3食欲差精神不佳 CT 31.35(+)；案例4黄疸食欲差 CT 30.96(+)；案例5腹部变大（腹水型）CT 22.75(+)Cats infected with FIP virus (FIPV); laboratory records show CT value changes before and after treatment in 5 cases: Case 1, low spirits, CT 33.1(+); Case 3, poor appetite and low spirits, CT 31.35(+); Case 4, jaundice and poor appetite, CT 30.96(+); Case 5, abdominal distension (ascitic form), CT 22.75(+).

处理方案Protocol

口服猫传腹病毒结合蛋白（福泰莱），疗程 7-15 次（对比传统方案 15 次以上，属短疗程）Oral administration of FIP virus receptor-binding protein (FuTaiLai), treatment course of 7–15 doses (compared with 15+ doses for conventional regimens, a short course).

效果Result

治疗后 5 例猫精神状态、食欲、腹水均好转，CT 值转阴（无 CT 值）或上升；资料称累计治好超过 200 只猫，且治愈后无反复、腹水吸收迅速After treatment, all 5 cats showed improvement in mental state, appetite and ascites, with CT values turning negative (no CT value) or rising; the data report over 200 cats cumulatively cured, with no relapse after recovery and rapid absorption of ascites.
