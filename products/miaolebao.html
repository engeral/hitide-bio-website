  1. 治疗（确诊或疑似 FPV/FHV/FCV）：每日 1 包（1.5g），混入食物或水中（或直接喂服），连续 7-10 天，配合兽医治疗方案使用Treatment (confirmed or suspected FPV/FHV/FCV): 1 sachet (1.5 g) daily, mixed into food or water (or given directly), for 7–10 consecutive days, used alongside the veterinarian's treatment plan.
