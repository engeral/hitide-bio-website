牛蛙 · 案例Bullfrog · Case

广东潮州牛蛙养殖场 · 细菌病暴发Chaozhou, Guangdong bullfrog farm · bacterial disease outbreak

背景Background

暴发细菌病，每天死亡 260-380 只，传统抗生素无效。Bacterial disease outbreak, 260–380 frogs dying per day; conventional antibiotics ineffective.

处理方案Protocol

改用蛙宝泰拌料投喂。Switched to WaBaoTai mixed into feed.

效果Result

迅速降低日死亡数量，细菌病得到控制。Rapidly reduced daily mortality; the bacterial disease was brought under control.

用法用量Directions for Use +
  1. 取本品 100g，加入 300ml 植物油充分混合后，用于拌料 20kg 蛙饲料使用Take 100 g of this product, mix thoroughly with 300 ml of vegetable oil, then use to coat 20 kg of frog feed.
  2. 连用本品 3-7 天Use this product for 3–7 consecutive days.
  3. 拌好的药饵不宜久置Prepared medicated feed should not be left standing for long.
  4. 不宜高剂量长期使用Avoid long-term use at high doses.
  5. 本产品仅适用于蛙用饲料添加This product is for frog feed additive use only.
是抗生素吗？Is it an antibiotic? +

是的，核心卖点是"反向包裹技术"——减少水体污染 + 减少耐药菌产生。Yes; the core selling point is 'reverse encapsulation technology' — reducing water pollution and resistant bacteria.

一包拌多少料？How much feed does one packet mix with? +

100g + 300ml 植物油 → 拌 20kg 蛙饲料。100g + 300ml vegetable oil → mixes with 20kg of frog feed.

跟蛙泰莱怎么选？How to choose between it and WaTaiLei? +

预防/慢病 → 蛙泰莱；急性/重症 → 蛙宝泰；可联合使用。Prevention/chronic → WaTaiLei; acute/severe → WaBaoTai; they can be used together.

用药后多久卖蛙？How long after use can frogs be sold? +

休药期待补充，请咨询技术团队。Withdrawal period to be supplemented; please consult the technical team.
