石斑鱼 · 案例Grouper · Case

山东东营 1.5 万尾石斑鱼 · NNV 攻毒Dongying, Shandong: 15,000 grouper · NNV challenge

背景Background

神经坏死病毒阳性，每天死亡 100+ 尾。Nervous necrosis virus positive, over 100 fish dying per day.

处理方案Protocol

连续拌料口服申泰莱 7 天。Administered ShenTaiLai orally in feed for 7 consecutive days.

效果Result

第 12 天后死亡数降低至 0 尾，神经坏死病毒病得到有效控制。By day 12, mortality dropped to 0 fish per day, and the nervous necrosis virus disease was effectively controlled.

鳜鱼 · 攻毒试验Mandarin Fish · Challenge Trial

NNV 攻毒保护试验 · 30 vs 30Mandarin fish · challenge test

背景Background

饲料鳜 NNV 攻毒，设对照组与治疗组各 30 尾，均肌肉注射 NNV。Feed-fed mandarin fish challenged with NNV, with a control group and a treatment group of 30 fish each, all intramuscularly injected with NNV.

处理方案Protocol

治疗组连续 5 天拌饲料口服 NNV 结合蛋白。The treatment group received NNV receptor-binding protein orally in feed for 5 consecutive days.

效果Result

治疗组死亡数显著低于对照组，5 天后存活率高于对照组，显示显著保护作用。Mortality in the treatment group was significantly lower than in the control group; survival at 5 days was higher than the control, demonstrating significant protection.

存活率显著优于对照Survival significantly better than control

用法用量Directions for Use +
  1. 取 1 包 A 包，加入约 600ml 水，边搅拌边加入，充分混匀至形成悬浊液Take 1 pack of Pack A, add to approx. 600 ml water while stirring, and mix thoroughly into a suspension.
  2. 与 20kg 饲料充分搅拌混匀Mix thoroughly and evenly with 20 kg of feed.
  3. 取 1 包 B 包（包裹材料）溶于约 600ml 水，边搅拌边加入至完全溶解Take 1 pack of Pack B (coating material), dissolve in approx. 600 ml water, adding while stirring until fully dissolved.
  4. 再加入上述 20kg 饲料中充分搅拌均匀完成包裹Add to the above 20 kg of feed and mix thoroughly and evenly to complete the coating.
  5. 每天投喂一餐，连续投喂 5-7 天Feed one meal per day for 5–7 consecutive days.
什么规格的石斑鱼用？What size of grouper is it for? +

商品规格可用。神经坏死病毒主要危害苗期（2-5cm），成鱼期也可用。Market-size fish can use it. NNV mainly affects fry (2-5cm), but adult fish can also use it.

连续用几天？How many consecutive days should it be used? +

说明书 5-7 天。山东案例用到第 7 天后死亡持续下降，第 12 天归零。Per label, 5-7 days. In the Shandong case, mortality kept dropping after day 7 and reached zero by day 12.

石斑鱼不吃料怎么办？What if grouper stop eating feed? +

需转人工，由技术团队评估。Manual intervention is needed; the technical team will evaluate.
