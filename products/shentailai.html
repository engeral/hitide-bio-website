石斑鱼 · 案例Grouper · Case

山东东营 1.5 万尾石斑鱼 · NNV 攻毒Dongying, Shandong: 15,000 grouper · NNV challenge

背景Background

神经坏死病毒阳性，每天死亡 100+ 尾。Nervous necrosis virus positive, over 100 fish dying per day.

处理方案Protocol

连续拌料口服申泰莱 7 天。Administered ShenTaiLai orally in feed for 7 consecutive days.

效果Result

第 12 天后死亡数降低至 0 尾，神经坏死病毒病得到有效控制。By day 12, mortality dropped to 0 fish per day, and the nervous necrosis virus disease was effectively controlled.

鳜鱼 · 攻毒试验Mandarin Fish · Challenge Trial

NNV 攻毒保护试验 · 30 vs 30Mandarin fish · challenge test

背景Background

饲料鳜 NNV 攻毒，设对照组与治疗组各 30 尾，均肌肉注射 NNV。Feed-fed mandarin fish challenged with NNV, with a control group and a treatment group of 30 fish each, all intramuscularly injected with NNV.

处理方案Protocol

治疗组连续 5 天拌饲料口服 NNV 结合蛋白。The treatment group received NNV receptor-binding protein orally in feed for 5 consecutive days.

效果Result

治疗组死亡数显著低于对照组，5 天后存活率高于对照组，显示显著保护作用。Mortality in the treatment group was significantly lower than in the control group; survival at 5 days was higher than the control, demonstrating significant protection.

什么规格的石斑鱼用？Which size of grouper is ShenTaiLai for? +

商品规格可用。神经坏死病毒主要危害苗期（2-5cm），成鱼期也可用。Market-size fish can be used. NNV mainly hits fry (2-5cm); adults can also be treated.

连续用几天？How many days? +

说明书 5-7 天。山东案例用到第 7 天后死亡持续下降，第 12 天归零。Label says 5-7 days; the Shandong case showed declining deaths from day 7, zero by day 12.

石斑鱼不吃料怎么办？What if grouper stops eating? +

需转人工，由技术团队评估。Escalate to human support for technical assessment.

申泰莱 / 仲泰莱 / 丹泰莱有什么区别？ShenTaiLai vs ZhongTaiLai vs DanTaiLai? +

三款均为病毒结合蛋白，适配病毒不同：申泰莱=神经坏死病毒（NNV，石斑/鳜鱼/鳗鱼），仲泰莱=传染性脾肾坏死病毒（ISKNV），丹泰莱=弹状病毒（Rhabdovirus）。一个产品对应一类病毒，不要混用。All are viral-binding proteins but for different viruses: ShenTaiLai=NNV (grouper/Mandarin fish/eel), ZhongTaiLai=ISKNV, DanTaiLai=Rhabdovirus. One product per virus class — do not mix.

石斑鱼神经坏死病毒怎么治？How to treat grouper NNV? +

主要危害 10cm 内鱼苗，发病 3-5 天死亡高峰。早期用申泰莱治疗效果较好。但 CT 值 25-30 可能是产品本身造成的检测干扰，建议用区分引物确认。Mainly harms fry under 10cm, death peak 3-5 days. Early ShenTaiLai works best. CT 25-30 may be product interference — confirm with differential primers.

AB 包为什么分两次拌？Why mix Pack A and B in two steps? +

A 包+B 包必须分两次拌：先 A 包 600ml 水悬浊液混入 20kg 饲料，B 包 600ml 水喷雾包裹。两包组合才能形成完整包裹，一次拌效果差很多。A+B must be mixed separately; both packs form the complete coating. One-step mixing is far less effective.

拌好的料一次用不完怎么办？What if mixed feed is left over? +

一定要当天用完。实在用不完，阴凉干燥处或冷藏保存，但必须避免饲料霉变，否则适得其反。Use the same day; otherwise store cool/dry or refrigerate but avoid mould.

海泰水产产品怎么保存？How to store the product? +

常温阴凉干燥处保存 12 个月，切勿高温暴晒；最佳储存温度 2-8℃（维持生物活性），能冷藏尽量冷藏。12 months cool/dry/shaded; best at 2-8℃.

CT 值多少可以治疗，多少不建议？Which CT value means treatable vs not? +

建议治疗区间 CT 值 25-30（最佳）；CT 值 <20（5 次方以上）+ 混合感染 + 采食量严重下降不建议治疗；CT 值 20-26 效果不明确。注意 CT 值 25-30 可能是产品本身检测干扰，建议用区分引物确认。Treatable CT 25-30 (best); CT <20 with co-infection/poor appetite not recommended; CT 20-26 unclear. CT 25-30 may be product interference.
