对虾 · 案例 01Shrimp · Case 01

中山虾 · 肝肠孢子虫 · 4 次方内Zhongshan shrimp · Enterocytozoon hepatopenaei (EHP) · within 10⁴

背景Background

虾体内肝肠孢子虫载量在 4 次方以内。Shrimp EHP load within 10⁴ (4th power).

处理方案Protocol

连续 7-8 天使用包泰莱拌饲料口服。Administered BaoTaiLai orally in feed for 7–8 consecutive days.

效果Result

7-8 天后虾体内肝肠孢子虫数量基本清零，摄食量与活力显著改善。After 7–8 days, EHP load in shrimp was essentially cleared, with markedly improved feed intake and vitality.

载量 4 次方 → 基本清零Load 10⁴ → essentially cleared
对虾 · 案例 02Shrimp · Case 02

珠海虾 · 治疗场景Zhuhai shrimp · treatment scenario

背景Background

肝肠孢子虫感染中后期。Mid-to-late stage of EHP infection.

处理方案Protocol

按体重计算剂量，连续投喂。Dose calculated by body weight, fed continuously.

效果Result

载量较用药前显著下降，采食量与活力逐步恢复。Parasite load dropped significantly versus pre-treatment, and feed intake and vitality gradually recovered.

载量 ↓ · 活力 ↑Load ↓ · Vitality ↑
对虾 · 案例 03Shrimp · Case 03

江苏小棚虾 · 预防方案Jiangsu small-shed shrimp · prevention program

背景Background

养殖密度高、疫病压力大。High stocking density and high disease pressure.

处理方案Protocol

放苗后定期投喂预防。Regular preventive feeding after stocking.

效果Result

感染率显著低于对照组，整体养殖成功率提升。Infection rate significantly lower than controls; overall farming success rate improved.

感染率 ↓Infection rate ↓
