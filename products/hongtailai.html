鳜鱼 · 案例Mandarin Fish · Case

佛山渔场 3 万尾鳜鱼 · RSIV 暴发Foshan fish farm: 30,000 mandarin fish · RSIV outbreak

背景Background

鳜鱼养殖暴发蛙虹彩病毒，用虹泰莱前每天死亡约 300 尾。A mandarin fish farm hit by ranavirus iridovirus; before using HongTaiLai, approx. 300 fish died per day.

处理方案Protocol

连续拌饲料口服 7 天。Administered orally in feed for 7 consecutive days.

效果Result

第 15 天后死亡数降低至 6 尾，蛙虹彩病得到有效控制。By day 15, mortality dropped to 6 fish per day, and the ranavirus iridovirus disease was effectively controlled.

300 → 6 尾/天300 → 6 fish/day
鲈鱼 · 案例Sea Bass · Case

鲈鱼虹彩病毒（CT 30.18）Sea Bass · Iridovirus (RSIV), CT 30.18

背景Background

虹彩病毒发病鲈鱼（CT 30.18），平均分 2 组，饲养条件一致。RSIV-infected sea bass (CT 30.18), split into two equal groups under identical husbandry.

处理方案Protocol

实验组投喂虹彩病毒结合蛋白包裹饲料，每天一餐连续 5 天；观察 7 天后检测。Trial group fed iridovirus binding-protein-coated feed, one meal daily for 5 consecutive days; tested after 7 days of observation.

效果Result

实验组 PCR 转阴（CT ≥45），平均存活率 65%；对照组仍为阳性（CT 31.668），存活率 35%。Trial group turned PCR-negative (CT ≥45) with average survival 65%; control remained positive (CT 31.668) with survival 35%.

甲鱼 · 案例Soft-shell Turtle · Case

一包能拌多少饲料？How much feed per pack? +

1 包 A 包 + 1 包 B 包配 20kg 饲料。1 Pack A + 1 Pack B per 20kg feed.

连续用几天？How many days? +

说明书 5-7 天。佛山案例用到第 7 天后死亡数持续下降，观察期到第 15 天。Label says 5-7 days; the Foshan case showed declining deaths through day 7, observed to day 15.

用后多久见效？When do results show? +

临床观察案例显示连续使用 7 天后死亡数明显下降。Clinical cases show a clear drop in deaths after 7 consecutive days of use.

用错了怎么办？What if used incorrectly? +

立即停止投喂，观察鱼体反应。Stop feeding immediately and observe the fish.

虹泰莱主要针对哪些鱼和病毒？Which fish and viruses does HongTaiLai target? +

主要用于鲈鱼（蛙虹彩病毒）、生鱼（蛙虹彩+弹状病毒）、鳜鱼（虹彩+脾肾坏死）、甲鱼（虹彩+黄病毒）等品种的虹彩病毒防控。Mainly largemouth bass (RSIV), snakehead (RSIV + Rhabdovirus), Mandarin fish (iridovirus + ISKNV), soft-shell turtle (iridovirus + flavivirus).

鲈鱼虹彩病毒怎么治疗？How to treat largemouth bass iridovirus? +

用虹泰莱连喂 5-10 天，每天 1 餐。最佳治疗区间 CT 值 25-30；CT 值 <26 或伴混合感染效果不明确。重症走“减料不减药”路线，至少 7 天。Feed HongTaiLai 5-10 days, one meal daily. Best window CT 25-30; CT <26 or with co-infection is unclear. Severe cases: reduce feed but not dose, at least 7 days.

鲈鱼虹彩病毒怎么预防？How to prevent largemouth bass iridovirus? +

苗期（40-50 日龄前）开口料连喂 10-14 天（虹泰莱+丹泰莱），之后每周 3 天；成鱼期首免 5 天，2 周后二免 3 天，之后每周 2 天。预防用药可减半（1 套拌 40kg 料）。Fry (before 40-50 days): 10-14 days from first feed (HongTaiLai + DanTaiLai), then 3 days/week; adults: 5-day primer, 3-day booster after 2 weeks, then 2 days/week. Prevention dose can be halved (1 set per 40kg feed).

AB 包为什么分两次拌？Why mix Pack A and B in two steps? +

A 包+B 包必须分两次拌：先 A 包 600ml 水悬浊液混入 20kg 饲料，B 包 600ml 水喷雾包裹。两包组合才能形成完整包裹，一次拌效果差很多。A+B must be mixed separately: dissolve Pack A in 600ml water into 20kg feed, then spray Pack B in 600ml water to coat. Both packs form the complete coating; one-step mixing is far less effective.

拌好的料一次用不完怎么办？What if mixed feed is left over? +

一定要当天用完。实在用不完，阴凉干燥处或冷藏保存，但必须避免饲料霉变，否则适得其反。Use the same day. If unavoidable, store cool/dry or refrigerate but avoid mould — moulded feed does more harm.

海泰水产产品怎么保存？How to store the product? +

常温阴凉干燥处保存 12 个月，切勿高温暴晒；最佳储存温度 2-8℃（维持生物活性），能冷藏尽量冷藏。12 months in a cool, dry, shaded place; avoid heat/sunlight. Best at 2-8℃ to keep bioactivity; refrigerate if possible.

CT 值多少可以治疗，多少不建议？Which CT value means treatable vs not? +

建议治疗区间 CT 值 25-30（最佳）；CT 值 <20（5 次方以上）+ 混合感染 + 采食量严重下降不建议治疗；CT 值 20-26 效果不明确。注意 CT 值 25-30 可能是产品本身检测干扰，建议用区分引物确认。Treatable window CT 25-30 (best); CT <20 (>=10^5 copies) with co-infection and poor appetite is not recommended; CT 20-26 is unclear. CT 25-30 may be product interference — confirm with differential primers.
