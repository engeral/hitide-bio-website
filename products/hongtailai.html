鳜鱼 · 案例Mandarin Fish · Case

佛山渔场 3 万尾鳜鱼 · RSIV 暴发Foshan fish farm: 30,000 mandarin fish · RSIV outbreak

背景Background

鳜鱼养殖暴发蛙虹彩病毒，用虹泰莱前每天死亡约 300 尾。A mandarin fish farm hit by ranavirus iridovirus; before using HongTaiLai, approx. 300 fish died per day.

处理方案Protocol

连续拌饲料口服 7 天。Administered orally in feed for 7 consecutive days.

效果Result

第 15 天后死亡数降低至 6 尾，蛙虹彩病得到有效控制。By day 15, mortality dropped to 6 fish per day, and the ranavirus iridovirus disease was effectively controlled.

300 → 6 尾/天300 → 6 fish/day
鲈鱼 · 案例Sea Bass · Case

鲈鱼虹彩病毒（CT 30.18）Sea Bass · Iridovirus (RSIV), CT 30.18

背景Background

虹彩病毒发病鲈鱼（CT 30.18），平均分 2 组，饲养条件一致。RSIV-infected sea bass (CT 30.18), split into two equal groups under identical husbandry.

处理方案Protocol

实验组投喂虹彩病毒结合蛋白包裹饲料，每天一餐连续 5 天；观察 7 天后检测。Trial group fed iridovirus binding-protein-coated feed, one meal daily for 5 consecutive days; tested after 7 days of observation.

效果Result

实验组 PCR 转阴（CT ≥45），平均存活率 65%；对照组仍为阳性（CT 31.668），存活率 35%。Trial group turned PCR-negative (CT ≥45) with average survival 65%; control remained positive (CT 31.668) with survival 35%.

存活率 65% vs 35%，CT 31.668 → ≥45Survival 65% vs 35%, CT 31.668 → ≥45
鲈鱼 · 案例Sea Bass · Case

鲈鱼虹彩病毒（高剂量组）Sea Bass · Iridovirus (RSIV), High-Dose Group

背景Background

虹彩病毒发病鲈鱼，平均分 2 组。RSIV-infected sea bass, split into two equal groups.

处理方案Protocol

实验组投喂虹彩病毒结合蛋白包裹饲料，每天一餐连续 5 天；观察 5 天后检测。Trial group fed iridovirus binding-protein-coated feed, one meal daily for 5 consecutive days; tested after 5 days of observation.

效果Result

实验组 PCR 转阴（CT ≥45），平均存活率 90%；对照组仍为阳性（CT 37.34），存活率 68%。Trial group turned PCR-negative (CT ≥45) with average survival 90%; control remained positive (CT 37.34) with survival 68%.

用法Directions
  1. 取 1 包 A 包，加入约 600ml 水，边搅拌边加入，充分混匀至形成悬浊液Take 1 pack of Pack A, add to approx. 600 ml water while stirring, and mix thoroughly into a suspension.
  2. 与 20kg 饲料充分搅拌混匀Mix thoroughly and evenly with 20 kg of feed.
  3. 取 1 包 B 包（包裹材料）溶于约 600ml 水，边搅拌边加入至完全溶解Take 1 pack of Pack B (coating material), dissolve in approx. 600 ml water, adding while stirring until fully dissolved.
  4. 再加入上述 20kg 饲料中充分搅拌均匀完成包裹Add to the above 20 kg of feed and mix thoroughly and evenly to complete the coating.
  5. 每天投喂一餐，连续投喂 5-7 天Feed one meal per day for 5–7 consecutive days.
疗程Course每天 1 餐，连续投喂 5–7 天；暴发期可延长至 7–10 天1 meal daily for 5–7 days; extend to 7–10 days during outbreaks
注意事项PrecautionsA 包溶于约 600ml 水后拌 20kg 饲料，B 包（包裹材料）等量水溶解后包被；现配现用，避免长时间放置Dissolve Pack A in ~600ml water and mix into 20kg feed; dissolve Pack B (coating) in equal water and coat; use fresh, do not store prepared feed
一包能拌多少饲料？How much feed does one packet mix with? +

1 包 A 包 + 1 包 B 包配 20kg 饲料。1 Packet A + 1 Packet B mixes with 20kg of feed.

连续用几天？How many consecutive days should it be used? +

说明书 5-7 天。佛山案例用到第 7 天后死亡数持续下降，观察期到第 15 天。Per label, 5-7 days. In the Foshan case, mortality kept dropping after day 7, with the observation period extending to day 15.

用后多久见效？How soon does it take effect? +

临床观察案例显示连续使用 7 天后死亡数明显下降。Clinical observation cases showed a marked drop in mortality after 7 consecutive days of use.

用错了怎么办？What if it is used incorrectly? +

立即停止投喂，观察鱼体反应。Stop feeding immediately and observe the fish's response.
