背景Background

免疫攻毒组（ISKNV 亚单位口服二免后）+ 攻毒对照组，各 30 尾，均肌肉注射 ISKNV。Immunized-challenge group (ISKNV subunit, two oral immunizations) + challenge-control group, 30 fish each, both intramuscularly injected with ISKNV.

鳜鱼 · 案例Mandarin Fish · Case

佛山 6.6 亩饲料鳜大塘口 · ISKNV 暴发Foshan 6.6-mu Mandarin Fish Pond · ISKNV Outbreak

背景Background

广东省佛山市 6.6 亩饲料鳜养殖场，ISKNV 阳性，死亡数急剧上升。A 6.6-mu feed-fed mandarin fish farm in Foshan, Guangdong; ISKNV-positive with rapidly rising mortality.

处理方案Protocol

第 7 天起每天一餐添加脾肾坏死病毒结合蛋白饲料，持续 7 天 + 后续 4 天补充投喂。From day 7, one meal daily with ISKNV binding-protein feed for 7 consecutive days, plus 4 additional days of supplementary feeding.

效果Result

第 26 天死鱼数量降至零，ISKNV 核酸检测阴性。By day 26 mortality dropped to zero and ISKNV nucleic-acid test turned negative.

用法用量Directions for Use +
  1. 取 1 包 A 包，加入约 600ml 水，边搅拌边加入，充分混匀至形成悬浊液Take 1 pack of Pack A, add to approx. 600 ml water while stirring, and mix thoroughly into a suspension.
  2. 与 20kg 饲料充分搅拌混匀Mix thoroughly and evenly with 20 kg of feed.
  3. 取 1 包 B 包（包裹材料）溶于约 600ml 水，边搅拌边加入至完全溶解Take 1 pack of Pack B (coating material), dissolve in approx. 600 ml water, adding while stirring until fully dissolved.
  4. 再加入上述 20kg 饲料中充分搅拌均匀完成包裹Add to the above 20 kg of feed and mix thoroughly and evenly to complete the coating.
  5. 每天投喂一餐，连续投喂 5-7 天Feed one meal per day for 5–7 consecutive days.
跟虹泰莱有什么区别？How does it differ from HongTaiLai? +

两款都是病毒结合蛋白类，但适配病毒不同：虹泰莱针对 RSIV，仲泰莱针对 ISKNV。Both are viral binding protein products, but they target different viruses: HongTaiLai targets RSIV, ZhongTaiLai targets ISKNV.

一包拌多少料？How much feed does one packet mix with? +

1 包 A 包 + 1 包 B 包配 20kg 饲料。1 Packet A + 1 Packet B mixes with 20kg of feed.

需要做 PCR 吗？Is PCR testing needed? +

建议做 PCR 确诊病毒种类再用药。It is recommended to confirm the virus type via PCR before use.

用错了怎么办？What if it is used incorrectly? +

立即停止投喂，观察鱼体反应。Stop feeding immediately and observe the fish's response.
