生鱼 · 案例Snakehead · Case

阳春 2 万尾生鱼 · 诺卡氏菌病Yangchun: 20,000 snakehead fish · nocardiosis

背景Background

250 克/尾，暴发诺卡氏菌病，每天死亡 130-150 尾，传统抗生素无效。250 g per fish, nocardiosis outbreak, 130–150 fish dying per day; conventional antibiotics ineffective.

处理方案Protocol

鱼宝泰每日 2 包拌 80 斤料。YuBaoTai, 2 packs daily mixed into 80 jin of feed.

效果Result

第六天后细菌病基本得到控制。By the sixth day, the bacterial disease was largely under control.

130-150 → 基本控制130–150 → largely controlled

用法用量Directions for Use +
  1. 取本品 100g，加入 300ml 植物油充分混合后，用于拌料 20kg 鱼饲料使用Take 100 g of this product, mix thoroughly with 300 ml of vegetable oil, then use to coat 20 kg of fish feed.
  2. 连用本品 3-7 天Use this product for 3–7 consecutive days.
  3. 拌好的药饵不宜久置Prepared medicated feed should not be left standing for long.
  4. 不宜高剂量长期使用Avoid long-term use at high doses.
  5. 本产品仅适用于鱼用饲料添加This product is for fish feed additive use only.
