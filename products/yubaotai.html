生鱼 · 案例Snakehead · Case

阳春 2 万尾生鱼 · 诺卡氏菌病Yangchun: 20,000 snakehead fish · nocardiosis

背景Background

250 克/尾，暴发诺卡氏菌病，每天死亡 130-150 尾，传统抗生素无效。250 g per fish, nocardiosis outbreak, 130–150 fish dying per day; conventional antibiotics ineffective.

处理方案Protocol

鱼宝泰每日 2 包拌 80 斤料。YuBaoTai, 2 packs daily mixed into 80 jin of feed.

效果Result

第六天后细菌病基本得到控制。By the sixth day, the bacterial disease was largely under control.

是抗生素吗？能用吗？Is it an antibiotic? +

氟苯尼考抗生素类，兽用处方药，凭兽医处方购买。Florfenicol, a veterinary prescription drug — purchase with a vet prescription.

一包拌多少料？How much feed per pack? +

100g + 300ml 植物油 → 拌 20kg 饲料。100g + 300ml vegetable oil → 20kg feed.

诺卡氏菌和普通细菌病怎么区分？How to tell Nocardia from ordinary bacteria? +

需 PCR 或镜检确诊，建议转人工。Confirm by PCR or microscopy; escalate to human support.

用药后多久可以卖鱼？When can fish be sold after use? +

休药期待补充，请咨询技术团队。Withdrawal period to be confirmed — contact the technical team.

鱼体出现混合感染（细菌+病毒+孢子虫）怎么办？What about mixed infection (bacteria+virus+microsporidia)? +

混合感染必须同时上对症组分：弧菌用抗菌蛋白 ABP，孢子虫用孢子虫结合蛋白（建议配精油），病毒用对应结合蛋白。强烈建议先做治疗验证，再用混合制剂。严重时窗口期很短。Address each component: ABP for Vibrio, microsporidia-binding protein (with essential oil) for microsporidia, the matching binding protein for virus. Validate with a treatment trial first.

对虾五合一方案含哪些组分？What is in the shrimp 5-in-1? +

海泰对虾五合一包括：肌肉坏死病毒结合蛋白 + 虾虹彩结合蛋白 + 白斑病毒结合蛋白 + 弧菌组分 + 孢子虫组分（可选）。技术层面多组分都能整合，成本远低于单组分分开。Muscle-necrosis-virus + iridovirus + WSSV binding proteins + Vibrio fraction + optional microsporidia fraction. Multi-component integration costs far less than separate products.

产品能和抗生素同时用吗？Can it be used with antibiotics? +

可以和抗生素合用，但要和强酸强碱氧化剂/消毒剂间隔 3 天以上。Yes, but keep ≥3 days apart from strong acids/alkalis, oxidizers or disinfectants.

拌好的料一次用不完怎么办？What if mixed feed is left over? +

一定要当天用完。实在用不完，阴凉干燥处或冷藏，但必须避免饲料霉变。Use the same day; otherwise cool/dry or refrigerate but avoid mould.

海泰水产产品怎么保存？How to store the product? +

常温阴凉干燥处保存 12 个月，切勿高温暴晒；最佳储存温度 2-8℃（维持生物活性），能冷藏尽量冷藏。12 months cool/dry/shaded; best at 2-8℃.
