背景Background

处理方案Protocol

效果Result

对虾体内虾白斑病毒含量清零（26 → 0 UI/ml；7 天清零）White spot syndrome virus load in shrimp cleared to zero (26 → 0 UI/ml; cleared in 7 days).

用法用量Directions for Use +
  1. 1. 取 1 包 A 包，加入到约 600ml 水中，边搅拌边加入使其充分混匀至形成悬浊液Take 1 pack of Pack A, add to approx. 600 ml water while stirring, and mix thoroughly into a suspension.
  2. 2. 与 20kg 饲料充分搅拌混匀Mix thoroughly and evenly with 20 kg of feed.
  3. 3. 取 1 包 B 包（包裹材料）溶于约 600ml 水中，边搅拌边加入至完全溶解Take 1 pack of Pack B (coating material), dissolve in approx. 600 ml water, adding while stirring until fully dissolved.
  4. 4. 再加入到上述 20kg 饲料中充分搅拌均匀完成包裹Add to the above 20 kg of feed and mix thoroughly and evenly to complete the coating.
  5. 5. 每天投喂一餐，连续投喂 5-7 天Feed one meal per day for 5–7 consecutive days.
