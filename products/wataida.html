用法Directions
  1. 取 2 包 A 包（500g），加入约 1200ml 水，边搅拌边加入，充分混匀至形成悬浊液Dissolve 2 packs of Pack A (500g) in ~1200ml water while stirring to form a uniform suspension
  2. 与 20kg 蛙饲料充分搅拌混匀Mix thoroughly with 20kg frog feed
  3. 取 2 包 B 包（包裹材料）溶于约 1200ml 水，边搅拌边加入至完全溶解Dissolve 2 packs of Pack B (coating) in ~1200ml water while stirring until fully dissolved
  4. 再加入上述 20kg 饲料中充分搅拌均匀完成包裹Add to the 20kg feed and mix evenly to complete coating
  5. 建议小四脚蛙苗时开始使用，首次连续投喂 3 天，每天一餐；间隔一个月后再投喂一次，连续 3 天，每天一餐Start at the small four-legged tadpole stage: first 3 consecutive days (1 meal/day), then repeat 3 days after a 1-month interval

蛙泰达适合什么情况？When is WaTaiDa used? +

针对牛蛙由气单胞菌、链球菌引起的红腿、烂皮、胃肠炎。For red leg, skin ulcer and gastroenteritis in bullfrog caused by Aeromonas / Streptococcus.

一包能拌多少饲料？How much feed per pack? +

取 2 包 A 包（共 500g）溶于约 1200ml 水，配 20kg 蛙饲料。Dissolve 2 packs of Pack A (500g total) in ~1200ml water, mix into 20kg frog feed.

连续用几天？How many days? +

建议小四脚蛙苗开始使用，首次连续 3 天；间隔 1 个月后再投喂一次连续 3 天。Start at small four-legged tadpole stage: 3 days, then repeat 3 days after 1 month.
